Philosophy

Clinical rigor, held gently.

We believe good therapy is both precise and human. Sessions draw on evidence-based approaches—ACT, REBT, and CBT where they help—and on what actually works for you. Come as you are: mid-thought, unsure, still figuring it out. That is not a prerequisite you need to meet before the work begins; it is the work. Therapy for young adults here is confidential, non-judgemental, and paced for you.

“Nothing about you needs fixing before you walk in. That is what the room is for.”

About Me

Neha Dukle

Psychotherapist · ACT, REBT & CBT · Young adults

My name is Neha Dukle — a psychotherapist offering evidence-based therapy for young adults. I work with an eclectic approach, trained in ACT and REBT, and drawing on CBT where it helps, because people rarely fit one method. I chose this niche because these 10–15 years—the quarter-life stretch—are when life transitions land: career shifts, relationships form and end, identities get rebuilt. Friends and family can hold a lot. But they’re not therapy. What this stage needs isn’t more advice, but a confidential, non-judgemental space where you can just be. That’s the room I try to hold.

Questions

A few things people ask.

Short answers about therapy for young adults, online counselling, and how to begin.

How do I know if I need therapy?

You don’t need a crisis. Therapy for young adults can help with life transitions, anxiety, relationship counselling, burnout, a quarter-life crisis, or a sense that something isn’t sitting right. If you want a confidential, non-judgemental space, a free 15-minute discovery call is a gentle place to start.

What happens in the first therapy session?

The first session is paced for you. We talk about what you’re navigating—anxiety, relationships, stress, or another life shift—and how evidence-based approaches such as ACT, REBT, or CBT might help. Online therapy sessions are available in Hindi and English, with flexible timings, or in person in Goa and Mumbai.

Is online therapy effective?

For many people, confidential online counselling is as useful as sitting in the same room. Online therapy pan-India means you can meet from home. In-person sessions are also available with a therapist in Goa (Panjim, Margao) and Mumbai (Borivali West).

Do you offer sessions in Hindi and English?

Yes. Online therapy sessions in Hindi and English are available across India, alongside in-person therapy in Goa and Mumbai.

How do I book a free discovery call?

Book a session via Google Form or message on WhatsApp to book a free 15-minute discovery call. After that, we can schedule ongoing therapy at a time that works for you—including evenings.
